GenView

Shaping Britain for those who will inherit it.

Heard less. Affected more.

From Votes at 16 to the ban on social media for under-16s, policy lands on the doorstep of young people more than ever. Yet the decisions that shape their lives are too often made without them.

The questions we'll ask

Every policy area shapes the lives of generations to come. Below are just some of the questions most affecting young people today.

  • Will today's young people be able to own a home?
  • What does Votes at 16 change about how politics listens?
  • How do we protect young people online without shutting them out?
  • Is the tax system fair to people just starting out?
  • Is mental health support built for the generation that needs it most?

How we'll answer them

GenView champions policy for the next generation. We publish research on the decisions that will define Britain for years to come, bring decision-makers face to face with the generation their choices affect most, and take the argument to the country.

GenView is in its pre-launch phase. Our first publication is due in 2027.
