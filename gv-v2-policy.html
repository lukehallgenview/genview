Why GenView ↓

Why it matters

The next generation is different. Young people are active well beyond the ballot box – in what they buy, the causes they back and the campaigns they join. From votes at 16 to the social media ban, government policy is landing on young people's doorstep more than ever before. Yet the decisions that will shape their lives for years to come are too often made without hearing them.

The questions we'll ask

Every policy area lands on the next generation. Below are just some of the issues most affecting young people today.

  1. Will the next generation be able to own a home?
  2. What does Votes at 16 change about how those in power listen?
  3. How do we protect young people online without shutting them out?
  4. Is the tax system fair to people just starting out?
  5. Is mental health support built for the generation that needs it most?

What we do

GenView champions policy for the next generation. We publish research on the decisions that will define Britain for years to come, bring decision-makers face to face with the generations their choices affect most, and take the argument to the country.

GenView is in its pre-launch phase. Our first publication is due in 2027.
