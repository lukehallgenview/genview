Policy for the next generation.

GenView champions policy for the next generation. We publish research on the decisions that will define Britain for years to come, bring decision-makers face to face with the generation their choices affect most, and take the argument to the country.

The next generation is different. Young people are active well beyond the ballot box – in what they buy, the causes they back and the campaigns they join. From votes at 16 to the social media ban, government policy is landing on young people's doorstep more than ever before. Yet the decisions that will shape their lives for years to come are too often made without hearing them.

Luke Hall
Director, GenView

Luke Hall is Director of GenView. His work spans roles in Parliament, industry and the third sector, and focuses on shaping the decisions that will define Britain, in the interests of those who will inherit it.
